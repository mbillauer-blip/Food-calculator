---
title: How Much Meat Per Person Do You Need for a Party?
description: Protein portion guidelines per adult and child guest, with adjustments for bone-in cuts, premium meats, and how many other dishes are on the table.
date: 2026-01-15
---

**The short answer:** plan for 115–200 grams (roughly 4–7 ounces) of raw protein per adult, and about 55% of that per child. Where you land in that range depends almost entirely on how many other dishes are on the table.

## Why portion size isn't a fixed number

A protein-only meal — one main, nothing else — needs a bigger portion than a spread with five sides and three carbs. As a rule of thumb:

- **0–1 other dishes on the table:** ~200g of protein per adult. The protein is the meal.
- **2–3 other dishes:** ~150g per adult. Guests are splitting attention across a few things.
- **4 or more other dishes:** ~115g per adult. This is buffet territory — people take a little of everything.

## Adjust for bone-in cuts

Bone-in cuts (chicken thighs, drumstick, wings, ribs, whole birds) need roughly **35% more raw weight** than the boneless equivalent, since a meaningful chunk of that purchased weight isn't edible meat. If a recipe calls for 150g of boneless chicken breast per person, budget closer to 200g for bone-in thighs to end up with the same amount of meat on the plate.

## Premium cuts disappear faster

Lamb, beef, steak, and other special-occasion proteins tend to get eaten in larger quantities than everyday chicken or fish — guests go back for seconds on the expensive stuff. Bumping the baseline portion up by **15–20%** for these cuts is a safer bet than sticking to the standard number and running short.

## Kids eat about 55% of an adult portion

This holds up well across appetizers, mains, sides, and carbs — it's a simpler and more accurate rule of thumb than assuming kids eat a fixed small amount regardless of the adult portion size.

## Rule of thumb, all together

| Scenario | Protein per adult |
|---|---|
| Single main, nothing else | ~200g |
| Main + 2–3 sides/carbs | ~150g |
| Buffet with 4+ other dishes | ~115g |
| Bone-in cut (any of the above) | ×1.35 |
| Premium cut — lamb, beef, steak | ×1.2 |
| Per child guest | ×0.55 of the adult number |
